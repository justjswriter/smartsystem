\section{Main API Endpoints}
\label{app:api-endpoints}

Table~\ref{tab:appendix-api-endpoints} summarizes the main API endpoints used in the implemented system.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Main API endpoints of the implemented system}
    \label{tab:appendix-api-endpoints}
    \small
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
    \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{
        >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.15\textwidth}
        >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.36\textwidth}
        >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X
    }
        \toprule
        \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
        \midrule
        POST & \path{/api/v1/auth/register} & Register a user account. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/auth/login} & Authenticate and obtain a JWT access token. \\
        GET & \path{/api/v1/auth/me} & Return the current authenticated user profile. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/plants} & Create a plant profile for the logged-in user. \\
        GET & \path{/api/v1/sensors} & List sensors visible to the current user. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/sensors} & Register a sensor and obtain a one-time device token; admin-only. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/sensors/{sensor_id}/assign} & Assign a provisioned sensor to a user; admin-only. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/sensors/{sensor_id}/attach} & Attach a sensor to a plant; admin-only. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/sensors/{sensor_id}/detach} & Detach a sensor from a plant; admin-only. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/sensors/{sensor_id}/rotate-token} & Rotate the device token and return a new one-time token; admin-only. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/ingest/sensors/{device_id}/data} & Submit sensor telemetry using \texttt{X-Device-Token}. \\
        GET & \path{/api/v1/dashboard/plants/{plant_id}} & Retrieve current values, history, condition, and recommendation. \\
        GET & \path{/api/v1/alerts} & Retrieve alerts available to the current user. \\
        GET & \path{/api/v1/notifications} & Retrieve persisted notifications for the current user. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/notifications/{notification_id}/read} & Mark one notification as read. \\
        POST & \path{/api/v1/notifications/read-all} & Mark all notifications of the current user as read. \\
        GET & \path{/api/v1/stream/alerts} & Access the alert SSE stream with ownership protection. \\
        GET & \path{/api/v1/stream/notifications} & Access the notification SSE stream with ownership protection. \\
        GET & \path{/api/v1/stream/dashboard/{plant_id}} & Access the plant dashboard SSE stream with ownership protection. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
